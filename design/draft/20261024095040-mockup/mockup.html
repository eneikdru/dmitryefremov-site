Raft Protocol & State Machine Replication

UPDATED Oct 24, 2024
READ TIME 8 min (1,840 words)
AUTHOR @d_vakil
INBOUND EDGES 18 Backlinks

The Raft consensus algorithm is designed to manage replicated logs across a distributed cluster. It decomposes state machine consensus into three discrete, mathematically independent problems: , log replication, and temporal safety invariants.

In steady-state operation, the elected leader handles client commands by writing them into a local append-only journal before fanning out RPC calls to active followers. In scenarios where a node degrades or partitions, it falls back to election timeouts to prevent split-brain states without requiring complete guarantees, operating strictly within crash-recovery semantics.

  uint64 leader_id = 2;
